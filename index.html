Quick Start
✝️
Nordic Cross 5 flags
Off-center cross shifted LEFT. Denmark is the original (~1370). Others just change the colors.
💡 Trick: Cross is always shifted LEFT. What color is the background? What color is the cross? That's all you need to know.
🟦
Vertical Tricolors 14+ flags
France (1789) invented this. The LEFT stripe is your main identifier.
💡 Trick: Cover the flag. What color is on the far LEFT? Green = African or Irish. Blue = French. Black = Belgium/Germany.
🟥
Horizontal Tricolors 14+ flags
Germany is anchor for Europe. Red-White-Black = Pan-Arab family. TOP stripe is the identifier.
💡 Trick: Red-White-Black = Middle East. What makes each different: the emblem, stars, or colored triangle.
🇬🇧
Union Jack Family 6 flags
Countries with historical ties to Britain. Union Jack = 3 crosses combined.
💡 Aus vs NZ: NZ = 4 red stars, no 7-point star. Australia = 6 stars including the big 7-pointer. "Bigger country = more stars."
☪️
Star & Crescent 12 flags
Islamic heritage symbol. Background color = main identifier.
🌍
Pan-African Colors 50+ flags
Green, Yellow/Gold, Red — inspired by Ethiopia (never colonized). Over 50 nations adopted these colors after independence.
💡 Trick: Learn Ethiopia, Ghana, Kenya as anchors. Then the SYMBOL or arrangement tells you which country. Nigeria = simplest (just green-white-green, no symbol).
🌙
Pan-Arab Colors 9+ flags
Red, White, Black, Green — from the 1916 Arab Revolt flag. Each color honors a historical Arab dynasty.
🔴
Red & White — Easy to Confuse! 10 flags
Just two colors — arrangement and shape make all the difference.
💡 Trick: Poland = WHITE top. Indonesia = RED top. Switzerland = SQUARE. Japan = red CIRCLE in center.
💙
Central American Blue & White 5 flags
Were ONE country (Federal Republic of Central America 1823–1841). Kept the same blue & white palette.
💛
Gran Colombia — Yellow, Blue, Red 3 flags
Colombia, Venezuela, Ecuador were ONE country until 1830. All share yellow-blue-red.
